Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 12, 2026

Data We Collect

Spendly collects only the information necessary to provide and improve the Service. This includes:

  • Account information — your name and email address when you register;
  • Expense data — the amounts, categories, dates, and descriptions you enter yourself;
  • Usage information — basic technical data such as your browser type, IP address, and pages visited, to keep the Service secure and working properly.

We do not collect sensitive financial information such as bank account numbers, passwords, or full card details. Spendly never sees or stores your banking credentials.

How We Use Your Data

We use the information we collect solely to operate, secure, and improve Spendly. Specifically, we use your data to:

  • Create and manage your account and authenticate your sessions;
  • Store and display the expense records you enter so you can review them;
  • Provide customer support and respond to your requests;
  • Fix bugs, resolve issues, and make the Service more reliable and useful;
  • Comply with legal obligations when required to do so.

We do not sell, rent, or lease your personal data to third parties for marketing purposes. We do not use your expense data for targeted advertising.

Data Storage

Your data is stored on secure servers operated by trusted hosting providers. We use industry-standard technical and organizational measures to protect your data from unauthorized access, disclosure, alteration, or destruction.

Your expense records are stored for as long as your account is active. If you delete your account, we will delete your personal data and expense records, except where we are required by law to retain certain information for a limited period.

No method of transmission or storage is perfectly secure, so while we take reasonable precautions, we cannot guarantee absolute confidentiality of any data you submit.

Third Party Services

Spendly may use limited third-party services for hosting, analytics, and error monitoring. These providers process data on our behalf under contractual obligations and are permitted to use it only for the purposes we specify.

These third parties may receive basic technical data such as your IP address, browser type, and usage patterns. They do not have access to your expense records or account credentials. We only engage providers who meet appropriate data protection standards.

Our pages may contain links to external websites. We are not responsible for the privacy practices of those sites, and we encourage you to read their policies before sharing any information.

Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, want to exercise your data rights, or have a privacy concern, please reach out through the contact information provided on the Service. We will make reasonable efforts to respond to your request.

You may also request deletion of your account and associated data at any time, subject to the retention exceptions described above.
